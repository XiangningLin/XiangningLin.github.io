I am fortunate to collaborate with Dr. Jiaxin Pei at Stanford HAI on system prompt auditing, and Prof. Ludwig Schmidt at Stanford CS on agent benchmarking.

Feel free to reach out if you have any ideas for potential collaboration, or just feel like having a casual chat!
