# Visitors

<p class="vis-note">
Where people reading this site are connecting from. Positions come from IP
geolocation, which resolves a country far more often than a city — so a visitor
on a VPN, a university or corporate network, or Apple's Private Relay lands in
the list as an unknown location rather than on the map. Those rows are readers
too, just ones whose address could not be placed.
</p>
